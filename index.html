KotlinJetpack ComposeMaterial 3MVVMLiteRTExecuTorchONNX RuntimeOpenCV
Explore the case study

Objective

Provide offline image, audio, video, and document processing without relying on cloud-based AI processing.

Google Play ↗

Implementation

Uses on-device inference with LiteRT (TensorFlow Lite), ExecuTorch, and ONNX Runtime. Models are downloaded on demand, so users can install individual capabilities without bundling large model files in the app.

Engineering focus

Mobile ML deployment, model optimization and quantization, hardware-accelerated inference, and multimedia processing, with OpenCV for image preprocessing and postprocessing.

Implemented tools

Background removal, AI photo enhancement, object removal, photo restoration, colorization, document scanning and OCR, speech-to-text, and audio noise reduction for audio and video. No planned tools are listed.
